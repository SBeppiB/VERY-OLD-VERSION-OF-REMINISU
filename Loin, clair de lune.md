“Do not be afraid, my child. The moon up above is the same moon you and me will be looking up at, even if I'm far away. “ was what papa used to say to me, every time I was scared of the lonely nights. He'd be on voyages to new lands, or hunting. But his quote always made me feel safe and close to him. 

However, recently, he had to go far away- to fight in some stupid war (again). And all that was left for me, was his tavern, and the moon.
