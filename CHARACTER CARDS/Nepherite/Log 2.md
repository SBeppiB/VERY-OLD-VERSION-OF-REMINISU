**Log  #2

TITLE: どこに行ったの...?

Objective: Remiニス

  

Date: 5/12/3456

  
  

Dear friend, where have you gone?

It feels like only yesterday we met at dawn

In the start, our bond was tight

But time slipped past, like jaded lights

  

I was your first, and now I am your last.

Through fleeting days, ones too fast to grasp

But you kept me company, ones strong and true

My gratitude will never fade for you

  

Forced to play in a sick, hopeless game

Their joy in watching suffering never waned

They called me 'Endling', a cursed, sad name

My violent fate, sealed in somber and shame

  

Have I wrong you? Do you despise me?

Bound in chains, I couldn’t be free

You cheered me on, gave me drive

My body coped with an overdrive

  

They murdered all, burnt your kind alive

For the first time, you and me let all hell loose

But in the end, it wasn't something we could choose

We achieved for the first time what we could not lose

  

I remember--

Your laboured breath

A wish to break free.

But I stood, I remained

A violence-bound beast, forever chained

  

A child’s soul, twisted in cold, iron veins,

Made to kill, but bound by unseen chains.  

Born from blood, but fueled by your light  

A hollow thing that aches for a fight



You gave me breath, but I gave you death  

In every strike, I stole your last breath  

I wasn’t built for mercy, nor for peace  


  

I wonder if I should have ever been born,

A creature made of rage, of sorrow, of scorn.

The legends, the myths

A rejecting world, truths hidden in a mist

But, you- a spirit glued me whole

Your warmth, your blood, a way to fill an empty hole

  

Yet through my eyes, I saw no grace,

Only war and greed, in a godless place.

No mercy shone, no kindness stayed,

Just endless suffering, love betrayed.

  

I miss your light, your voice, your charm,

Your touch, once soft, now distant, far.

Constant bloodshed kept my shell alive

My creators wonders let me survive

But your soul… it lets me thrive.

  

Tree leaves

Free leaves

On my knees

I begged, I plead

  

For your soul protected me

It nourished me

When all I could see was…

…a sea of suffering destiny

  

Friend, friend? Oh friend of mine,

I know, you’ve shown

Your hollow thoughts left long ago

When I found I could take another's soul

Your pain and suffering

Is something I will truly never know

  

I hoped, I dreamed

I could make things right

Not to end with a fight,

Nor leave a world in blight

  

I stood motionless

in a field, no soul in sight,

I did what was right

  

In front of a tree so tall

Our last season together

In the fall

A gloomy, dark weather

  

Refused to put up a fight

I cried, and cried

When I tore you apart

Ended your suffering 

On that final night
