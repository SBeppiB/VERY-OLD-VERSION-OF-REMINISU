## Chapter 1 - Corvo cieco

  
  

In the Scari household, girls never cried. They straightened their backs, carried words like a weapon, and wielded sharp wisdom and a sense of justice. Rena was not an exception, born into a long line of razor sharp women, she wasn't raised on lullabies, bribes, lies or candy, but on harsh expectations. Her mother never believed in weakness, her grandmother believed even less. “Le emozioni ti rendono cieca,” they’d say, “emotions make you blind”.

  

But Rena was full of it.

  

Rena Scari used to be the loudest voice in the rooms she wandered, never backing down, always seeking truth and justice as a younger person. She was literally the type to walk straight into a room full of problems and dared to punch first, even as a teen- her tongue was sharp and even she had sharper instincts. Teachers called her confrontational, troubled, friends looked up to her, calling her fearless. Enemies never found words to call her, not even the greatest poets whom she met could. She’d already gotten too close, figured them out, and left them questioning themselves. 

  

She would talk her way into, through, and out of anything. 

Got caught in the wrong place? “Helping a friend”

Dragged into school fights? “Just removing a problem”

  

Confrontation never slowed her, only lies did. She was quite social, always making friends with strangers, and enemies into unlikely allies, all with the reckless fire she wore with pride. Her tactics never failed her, it was her master strategy. Her honesty was her sharpest weapon, and for a long time, it always worked. 

  

But, the world did not play kindly with Rena. 

  

The house was always cold, despite the Italian sun leaking through the windows. Three generations of women under one roof, and none of them ever said “I love you.” But god, they expected everything from her. Perfection, grace, control.

  

When the Scari house got too cold for her liking, Rena searched for warmth elsewhere. Rooftops under starry skies, where the town smelt like smoke and broken dreams. She ran with the kind of hooligans who smiled like they’d been punched in the face one too many times—rebels, thieves, ghosts with nothing left to lose.

They didn’t ask questions. They didn’t judge.  
They just were.

One of her first friends was a blind girl, around two years older than Rena, named Eleni. Rena never understood how blindness worked, but who with sight and was never blind really knows? They met in a park when they were 3, and Rena called out on how Eleni would talk to the trees every morning. Eleni was always the kind one though, and Rena just stuck with her ever since, as Eleni never judged Rena. 

Eventually, when Rena and Eleni were 4 and 6 respectively, Seraphina, aged 7 was forced to meet them as their dads were childhood friends separated by war and Seraphina was a hot mess, but was the life of the party. She’d teach them cool tricks like pick pocketing, blending in with crowds and even taught them how to fight dirty, but Eleni was blind, so most of the dirty work was done by Rena, with Eleni being emotional support. However, despite their friendship, Eleni’s mother would always keep her away from straying off for too long, especially with hooligans like Rena and Seraphina, ESPECIALLY SERAPHINA.

Soon enough, more people joined, but the trio was permanent, nothing would break em apart. Some stayed, some left, but none ever snitched.

Clubbing in dank areas till dawn, blazing in the dark through old dusty alleys that smelled like piss and regret, stealing moments that felt like freedom. This was Rena’s real family. The one that didn’t silence her for who she was. But every high had its crash. Every rooftop fall left bruises. And some friends she made just vanished. One by one. Leaving Rena to her own thoughts. Thoughts that were absolutely wild, she never told anyone, not even in prayers, just something going down with her to her grave

Her world was a museum of the old world humanity had flown into space from, never forgotten, but never talked about. Barely any news came from earth- every civilisation and every human inhabited planet just whispered folktale and stories that came from this mudball. Everything was left to be preserved, and not many roam the cities anymore, and the few that do are often rich or unfortunate to have been born in a Sheeshhole in someone's slum. Money was kind of harder to get now that everything costs too much, whether it be that new synthetic implant every kid in school wants to get from their local illegal SyDoc, or a house rumored to have untold treasures. Money was never owned, only used in turns.

Another fun memory of hers was when Eleni finally had her sight restored when she turned 16 as a surprise gift, as her mother finally saved enough money. She remembered how her heart pounded when Eleni saw her for the first time, oh the rush. Even Seraphina shed a tear, and that girl laughed at a blind and deaf dog falling down a 14 flight concrete staircase

One hazy, starry night, by the time she was 14 when her voice cracked less, but her fire burnt hotter, she and the remaining of her gang were out on a stealing spree. All for some extra cash so they could buy a Cinquedea of their own, for “fun” purposes.
