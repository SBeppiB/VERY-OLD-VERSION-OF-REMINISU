Chapter 1

**OUTLINE**

rena and her friends seraphina and eleni are hanging out bored at Seraphina's cousin's place. rena suggests they steal valuables from rich people to earn money for a blue obsidian cinquedea dagger from the black market. they target some rich kids and their grandmother, successfully stealing phones, rings, headphones and a locket. the grandmother's grandson Gianni chases them across Florence rooftops. they escape into an alley and discover an abandoned bank that was supposedly burned down during riots over a $500 million ancient mask. they decide to break in and steal it. seraphina disguises herself as a janitor to get past security. rena accidentally kills a security guard by knocking a computer onto him while hiding. they use a usb device to fry the security systems. they navigate down to the vault using the bank's own virtual tour as a guide. rena hacks the vault door open. they find and steal the mysterious mask. they escape with it and trade their stolen goods at a pawnshop for the cinquedea dagger. eleni steals a car for their getaway. police investigate afterward but never find the culprits.

**CHARACTER DYNAMICS**

rena is the bold confident leader who talks her way through everything until she accidentally commits her first murder and freezes up. seraphina is the experienced cynical one who's done shady deals before and stays calm even during the killing. eleni is the reluctant moral compdonkey who follows along but questions their choices. the three have solid friendship chemistry built on years of petty crime together. Rena's family (scari household) is emotionally cold and demanding, pushing her to seek warmth and acceptance from her street crew instead.

**KEY EVENTS**

the group successfully robs the rich kids and grandmother. Gianni chases them but they escape. they discover the abandoned bank. seraphina talks her way past a security guard. rena accidentally kills the guard with a falling computer. seraphina sabotages the security system. they navigate to the vault following a virtual tour. rena hacks open the vault door. they steal the ancient mask which whispers strange things. they escape and trade goods for the cinquedea at a pawnshop. eleni provides a stolen getaway car.

**FILLERS**

banter between the three friends establishing their personalities and relationships. rena's internal conflict about her cold family vs her chosen family. the sensory details of florence at night - rooftops, alleys, the bank's eerie atmosphere. seraphina smoking and making dark jokes. eleni being the voice of reason. the pawnshop owner's cryptic warning about bringing storms.

**REFINE**

after successfully robbing the kids and escaping Gianni, the trio discovers the abandoned bank with the legendary mask. seraphina infiltrates as a janitor while rena enters through vents. when a guard discovers them, rena accidentally kills him with a falling computer and experiences shock at her first kill. seraphina stays pragmatic and destroys the security systems. they follow the bank's virtual tour to locate the vault. rena hacks it open and they steal the mask despite its unsettling whispers. they escape, trade their loot for the cinquedea dagger, and vanish before police arrive. the mask and locket both seem supernaturally active, roll credits end of chapter 1 blah blah blah
