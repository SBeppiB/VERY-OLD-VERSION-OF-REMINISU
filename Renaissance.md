the core battery:

"does this require someone else to be less to function" the original. still the best one for ideological stuff

"would this still be okay if it was pointed at me" flips the perspective fast. cuts through a lot of bullshit reasoning people use to justify things

"is the harm direct or imagined" because some people claim harm when they just mean discomfort or disagreement. renaissants should know the difference

"who benefits from me staying neutral here" sometimes neutrality actively helps the wrong side. if your silence is someone else's weapon that's not neutrality anymore that's complicity wearing a philosophy costume

"is this about what someone IS or what someone DOES" targeting existence = tier one automatic. targeting behaviour = potentially still grey area worth questioning

"can both sides of this exist at the same time without one destroying the other" if yes — genuine grey area, stay curious if no — somebody's trying to erase somebody else, not neutral

the meta question that sits above all of them: "am i staying neutral because i genuinely don't know yet or because taking a stance is uncomfortable"

- knows the ideology basics
    
- carries a notebook + writing instrument
    
- has a bag
    
- aware of morse, braille, ASL — even if not fluent yet
    

---

## tier 1 — the apprentice (pencil + pen)

you've started doing the work.

- functional in at least one unconventional communication medium
    
- actively learning something outside your comfort zone
    
- has a reference card on them
    
- can explain the battery questions without looking them up
    

---

## tier 2 — the joat (pen, confident)

jack of all trades. genuinely skilled across multiple areas.

- functional in all three core mediums (morse, braille, ASL)
    
- has a visible genre blend that's intentional not accidental
    
- maintains a consistent marginalia habit
    
- is actively teaching or sharing knowledge with others (altruistic part kicks in here)
    

---

## tier 3 — the polymath (fountain pen energy)

this is the apex. not many get here.

- ambidextrous or actively training it
    
- has created something original using the adapt mimic create framework
    
- is a knowledge vein for their community
    
- their emotional redirection is visible in their output — art, writing, music, whatever
