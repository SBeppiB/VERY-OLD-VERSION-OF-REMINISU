## 9. NON-DISCLOSURE AND TAKEDOWN PROCEDURES

### 9.1 Takedown Request Process

#### 9.1.1 When Creator May Request Takedown

- Content violates terms in Section 3.1 (zero tolerance violations)
- Content falsely claims to be official
- Content infringes on reserved rights (Section 6.1)
- Content causes significant harm to Creator's reputation
- Content violates third-party rights
- Official adaptation requires fan game removal

#### 9.1.2 Notification Method

Creator will send takedown notice via:

- Direct message on platform where content is hosted
- Email to contact provided by fan creator
- Public comment on content (last resort)

Notice will include:

- Specific content in violation
- Relevant section of this document
- Requested action (removal, modification, age-restriction, etc.)
- Deadline for compliance (72 hours standard)

#### 9.1.3 Fan Creator Response Requirements

Upon receiving takedown notice, fan creator must:

- Acknowledge receipt within 24 hours
- Comply with request within 72 hours
- May request clarification or appeal (see Section 9.2)
- Must remove ALL copies across ALL platforms

### 9.2 Appeal Process

#### 9.2.1 Grounds for Appeal

- Belief that content does not violate guidelines
- Technical impossibility of compliance
- Extenuating circumstances requiring extension
- Miscommunication or mistaken identity

#### 9.2.2 Appeal Submission

- Submit to: sbeppibubtalz@gmail.com
- Subject: "Takedown Appeal - [Content Title]"
- Include: Explanation, evidence, proposed solution
- Deadline: Within 48 hours of takedown notice

#### 9.2.3 Appeal Review

- Creator will review within 5 business days
- Decision is final unless new evidence emerges
- If appeal denied, original deadline stands
- If appeal approved, new terms may be negotiated

### 9.3 Consequences of Non-Compliance

#### 9.3.1 Strike System

**Strike 1 (First Violation):**

- Formal warning issued
- Grace period extended to 7 days
- Opportunity for education on guidelines

**Strike 2 (Second Violation):**

- Exclusion from official community showcases
- Exclusion from future collaboration opportunities
- Permanent record of violation

**Strike 3 (Third Violation):**

- Legal action may be pursued
- Permanent ban from official community spaces
- Public disavowal of fan creator's works
- DMCA takedowns filed on all platforms

**Strike 4 (Fourth Violation):**

- Cease and desist letter issued
- Potential lawsuit for damages
- Permanent blacklist from any future fan content creation

#### 9.3.2 Strike Expiration

- Strikes expire after 2 years of compliant behavior
- Violent or illegal content violations do not expire
- Violations involving minors do not expire

#### 9.3.3 Immediate Escalation

Following violations skip directly to Strike 3:

- Sexualization of minors
- Doxxing or harassment
- Malicious impersonation of Creator
- Fraudulent commercial activity using IP

Permitted with care:

- Age swap (adults as teens, teens as adults - NO sexual content involving aged-down characters)
- Species swap (human/supernatural - NO furry content)
- Profession swap

### 12.2 AU Restrictions

#### 12.2.1 Character Integrity Requirements

When creating AUs, must maintain:

- Core personality traits that define character
- Fundamental relationships (rivals remain rivals, family remains family, etc.)
- Character motivations and goals (adapted to new setting)
- Physical appearance recognition (unless appearance change is the point)

#### 12.2.2 Age Modification Limits

- Cannot age down adult characters more than 5 years for sexual content purposes
- Cannot age up minor characters for sexual content purposes
- Time-skip AUs showing natural aging: Permitted
- De-aging for non-sexual storytelling: Permitted

#### 12.2.3 Gender/Sexuality Changes

- Gender-swapped AUs: Permitted
- Must be respectful and not rely on stereotypes
- If character has canonical sexuality, changes should be thoughtful
- Trans/non-binary headcanons and AUs: Permitted
- Must not be fetishizing or disrespectful

#### 12.2.4 Prohibited AU Types

Explicitly prohibited:

- Furry AUs (anthropomorphic animal versions)
- AUs that fundamentally contradict core character identity beyond recognition
- AUs created solely to bypass content restrictions (e.g., aging up minor solely for NSFW)
- AUs that promote hate speech or harmful ideologies

### 12.3 AU Quality Expectations

#### 12.3.1 Originality Requirements

Strong AUs should include:

- Original plot developments, not just rehashed canon in new setting
- Thoughtful adaptation of character dynamics to new context
- World-building appropriate to AU setting
- Logical consequences of AU changes

Weak AUs (discouraged but not prohibited):

- Character names copied into new setting with no personality adaptation
- No consideration of how setting would affect characters
- Copy-paste of canon dialogue with no adaptation
- "Just wanted to see characters in this aesthetic" with no depth

#### 12.3.2 Crossover AU Considerations

When combining AU concept with crossover:

- Must respect both source materials
- Must credit both IPs
- Must follow guidelines of both properties if applicable
- See Section 7 for full crossover guidelines
