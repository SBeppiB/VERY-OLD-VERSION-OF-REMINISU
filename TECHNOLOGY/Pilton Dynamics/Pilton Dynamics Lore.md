Pilton dynamics is a company which was ran by the Pilton bloodline. The founder- Thomas Pilton born 31st August 1981 was a young child, raised by a dad who was a prominent immigrant Mechanic based in New York, America. His father taught Thomas how to fix his first bike when he was 4, and even helped him learn every part of a Suzuki GSX1100S Katana and how to put one together blindfolded

  

After reading the newspaper on robotics, artificial intelligence and machinery one late December afternoon in 1994, Thomas, the little mechanical tweaker he was, wanted to stick some screws and wrenches into some new technology. Thomas learnt how to make his own mechanical creations, and then discovered programming languages. He created tiny robots like rovers and cars out of scrap, programming them and even using a small narrow AI to detect and go towards targets.

  

Thomas’s scrapyard bots caught local eyes, a street race for the kids his age here. A mob job there. By 2001, he’s selling illegally modified drones to shady fixers. When the police sniff him and his crew out, he flips the script and says that his works are for a defense tech startup and that he was “building his portfolio”. All while building a Mafia boss complex and a reputation of the most stacked drug dealer.

  

6 Years later, on a drive to get more resources like parts for Thomas’s robots and for his dad to use, a gangoon squad shot em up in a drive-by, fatally wounding his dad and badly injuring Thomas. The gangoons were never found, and his family didn't have enough to pay for his dad’s insurance. With his dad gone and almost everything, Thomas swore no one in the pilton household will ever be hurt again, he wired vengeance into every creation he made. Every AI, every robot, every blueprint: “Never let your kin suffer”

  

So after his little dark age, he creates the Sentinel Zetta, a prototype combat bot with a rigged AI chip. It went berserk in his lab, flattening half of it. Then it went on a rampage, grabbing firearms and shooting up the homeless in 2009. Zetta was solar powered, and was last seen in God knows where it went, its last sighting was in a desert far away from home. Probably dumped into the ocean nearby, probably nuked from orbit. 

  

Mid 2012, after almost getting his tech den’s digital footprints sniffed out by Yarako’s security dogs (not actual dogs), Thomas decided that Yarako Dynamics was a large nuisance in people’s day to day life and didn't like how they were patrolling a lot of things. So he sought to slowly annoy them bit by bit. And with this, and the intent of stopping people from getting abused without knowing, he created Pilton Dynamics. Thomas didn't know where to start off at first, but he did have a sort of “crime” family tagging along behind him, his first clients helped him afford the resources he needed and soon he rented a warehouse outside New York to fully produce guerilla AI’s, more combat machines and weaponry.

  

He met a wonderful business woman during a meeting with an international security and fight regulation council, her name was alaya The two got into a seriously intimate relationship and they both had two boys, named Trevor and Charlie Pilton. She unfortunately died from complications during childbirth, and wrecked with grievance and too much work, Thomas relied on his father to take care of the children.

  

In 2024, during the rise of supposed “neuralink” technology, Thomas and his team of developers in the outskirts of New York saw how it could help the disabled, paralysed and unfortunate people of the world. So they attempted to replicate it and make their own, one that would be able to hijack and hack into systems, inject viruses, add files, and control what the admin does. Soon, their invention worked, and they kept the tech to themselves, not even willing to sell it to the public. 

  

Thomas knew that the technology would be able to wreak havoc on their world, their world that wasn't made ready to handle it. But his thirst for discovery could never be quenched, so he implanted the modified technology into himself.

  

Unfortunately for him, the chip was never regulated, or even made to last, so his chances of survival were slim, and unfortunately for Yarako, Thomas was never one to die out slowly, no. His flame did not yearn for a slow death, it wanted to go out in a blast. He made countless prototypes to try and lengthen his lifespan but only got 110 years left. Alas, the thought of living for 110 years felt like a curse, and Thomas’s mental strain was too much to handle

  

So, at midnight in christmas 2133, him and a rag tag team from a pilton dynamic’s loyal volunteers campaign armed to the teeth with the military offensive weaponry of a small town and superhuman senses blasted into the rooftop of Yarako Dynamic HQ in Iceland, breaking through the 4 highest floors, and planted 16 magnetic EMP nukes into the server rooms, security and production lines. He then dropped a prototype nuclear warhead said to have the “Power to skin an island of its flesh, and expose its blood to the world” in an elevator, and shot the chains. 

  

No one really knew what killed Thomas first, the guard’s gunfire, or being crushed under rubble. But the legend still stands even a thousand years later, that Thomas Pilton and his gangoon were the boogeymen of Yarako Dynamics, and that no other man would accomplish what he has done. Note: Man. 

  

Charlie and Trevor grew up and followed his fathers footstep, becoming a professional biotech engineer, and decided to run pilton dynamics with an iron fist, built its HQ on top of the ruins where Yarako used to stand. Now, with a company dedicated to researching combat robotics, and bioengineering, Trevor grew hungrier for more power. And in 2077, he made a sub branch of Pilton Dynamics called Roota Industries, the company which would manufacture war machines and weapons instead, making Pilton Dynamics focus all of its resources into more technology that would advance space exploration.

  

Charlie on the otherhand, wanted to expand Pilton Dynamic’s domain. So he created advanced lunar and extraterrestrial missions to advance his fathers company further
