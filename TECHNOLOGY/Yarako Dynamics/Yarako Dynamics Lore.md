Yarako Dynamics was a company ran by the Yarako Bloodline. The founder- Kioku Yarako (記憶やらこ), born 11th december 1985, was a young teenager who was a prodigy in technology in the year 2001, after the 9/11 attacks, Kioku watched from his grandfather’s TV how fragile large systems were and saw how the west’s obsession with technology skyrocket. After seeing how people panicked, failed to stop and predict the attack of 9/11、 Kioku thought “人為的ミス。完璧なマシンに交換してください.” He was obsessed with the concept of autonomous security、 drones, bots, and surveillance AI.

  

The weeks following the 9/11 attacks, Kioku was in underground forums before turning them into remote labs, shut off from the public. Their basement bots became city-scale security networks. 

  

By the time Kioku was 19 they decided to join Tokyo Guard and Security Co., Ltd for steady pay and to support their family. Unfortunately for the Yarako family, Kioku was fired from their job 6 weeks after, with their employer deeming them to be creating “Inhumane creations.” 

  

Being an independent developer, a member of a struggling household and a drop-out, Kioku decided that they had more unsolved business. They started making smart surveillance grids for the USA and were prominent trader, making more tech like:  
  

Anti-intrusion drones for megacorps.  
  

Adaptive surveillance grids for riot control.

  

In 2015, youtube rose up in popularity, and with it, people smarter than Kioku posted videos on how to do better. How to make better tech, more concepts, efficient AI’s. Kioku watched hundreds of videos and learnt hundreds times more than they did before

  

Soon, more tech kicked in, and Yarako became the lead supplier of surveillance security. But no one knew their real face, gender or even where they’re actually from. Kioku was hidden behind other greedy corps, and still made the big bucks. At the age of 27, they had their first children, twins, Hinansho (避難所) and Jihi (慈悲)

  

The first modern AI’s, ones that would learn effectively from their mistakes, in the 2020’s fascinated Kioku. “Robots that can learn from mistakes?” they thought And with this- in 2016, Yarako Dynamics was formed. A small company, yes, but they only needed a team of 300 software developers and a low profile to keep creating more tech.

  

Core Tech Divisions

Sentinel AI: Predictive surveillance systems that don’t just monitor—they anticipate crimes, protests, even rebellions before they happen. They’re wired into city infrastructure, traffic cams, personal devices—every pixel feeds them data.

  

Black Ops R&D: The dark heart. Experimental combat bots with self-learning AI, rumored to have adaptive combat tactics that evolve mid-fight. These don’t just kill—they dominate.

  

NeuroNet Interface: An experimental brain-computer interface designed to sync operators’ minds with AI systems, boosting reflexes and battlefield awareness. The future? Human-machine fusion where Kioku’s tech doesn’t just watch but controls human will.

  

These creations were the first of many, and Kioku wasn't just a surveillance robotics engineer- no, they were the largest data farmers for the AI boom in the early 2020’s, and one of the most prominent person who convinced the UK government to put a “Child Safety Act” on all websites in the 25th of july 2025, claiming it would “protect children from seeing harmful content on the internet”. People were forced to show their ID’s and face images to a bot and then the data would be harvested and used to train AI’s for upcoming facial recognition tests. These would be then used for the first AI’s to watch and comprehend videos. It was genius, simple facial changes being compiled into videos.

  

After the apparent “Death of Kioku” which took place in the year 2027, their twin children, who were 15 at the time, decided to not let their parent’s legacy go to waste. They used Kioku’s wealth to develop more bots, and make them more wild. Security drones emerged in the early 2030’s and no one dared to stop the twins, especially not with two other security robotic guards by their sides. Yarako did not only become a surveillance robotics company, no, it became a weaponry and heavy artillery security force for other smaller companies.

  

Space travel boomed in 2041, especially for lunar mining, asteroid mining and planet exploration. More resources, more competition, more war. Yarako found its golden opportunity and took it. They started off by helping corps set up their mining rigs in the moon, then helped deflect some asteroids hurdling towards the moon and even from earth. Then, other space objects like mining small moons or asteroids for resources.

  

In the year 2079, Yarako Dynamics was situated on Mars. Mars had a lot of valuable resources so Yarako partnered with the smaller Hezi Mining corp to mine and sell the resources, in exchange for better tech like rigs, software, protection, etc.

  

Eventually, two geniuses by the alias Melara (David mechel and Lara crasse) stumbled upon a pictogram from an ancient meteor impact crater site. This pictogram was trapped between a thin layer of rocks, so Melara studied it and found the earliest form of magic technology, and margian crafts, as well as studies. 

  

These were likely remnants of asteroid bits that hit Mars as it was sent to earth 65 million years ago. Since Melara were the first humans in 65 million years to learn about magic, they started integrating it to machines and eventually found that all life has a soul, regardless of its animate, animal, plant, or human. So therefore, it has some magical capabilities. Humans evolved on a planet without magic or “ARC” matter (Arcanium), so their souls weakened due to the lack of usage in magic.

  

Due to this discovery, they conducted thousands of tests on the soul, its purpose, usage, extent, even causing indirect catastrophic events, even discovering resurrection due to life berries and crystals. After accidentally leaving a soul next to a USB connected to a machine intended to record frequencies for over a week, Melara came across a monitor connected to the machine displaying the soul’s memories.

  

The USB, too, was found overloaded with the memories, 265GB all used up in the span of 2 seconds. 

  

This prompted them to lead more research on this new discovery. Souls can contain memories…?

  

Soon, they invented the Mneme drive, a machine capable of replaying memories via video or AI generated outputs (The AI watches it and generates a description gets 99.9% accurate descriptions)

  

Yarako Dynamics picked up on this and made their sick little twist- a machine similar to the mneme drive, took its capturing memory ability, and modified it to capture consciousness. Living souls still in the body possess the consciousness of the being, so they took both David and Lara’s bodies and trapped them in the machine, which’s called the Starsis chamber.

  

Pilton Dynamics crossed their paths, for one of the last times, and waged war against Yarako after executing one of their top engineers for blueprints of a war model. Many countries tried to stop them, but most of their combat machines came from Pilton Dynamics, so after cutting off supplies to opposing countries, Pilton Dynamics neutralised ONLY hostile forces coming towards their paths, and then sent hundreds of their special forces to surround and capture any survivors coming from Yarako.

  

However, in the year 2133 on christmas evening, after successfully blowing up a pilton dynamic bioengineering facility, the founder of Pilton Dynamics, Charlie, and a ragtag team of pilton dynamic’s loyal volunteers armed to the teeth with the military offensive weaponry of a small town and superhuman senses decided that enough was enough and kamikazee’d into Yarako HQ in Iceland.

  

And just like that, Yarako Dynamics fell.
