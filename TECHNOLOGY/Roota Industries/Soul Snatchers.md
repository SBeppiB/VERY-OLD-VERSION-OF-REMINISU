Lore:

  

Soul snatchers are robots created for war in the Origin Universe, during the colonisation of Gupar. Their original design was somewhat of a mistake- during the creation of the END project, they encountered a bug with the machine engine, and in February 18th 3421, the prototypes or SSV1(SoulSnatcher), were created by roota industries and were soon mass produced to be universally spread through factions such as StellarHezi, Pilton Dynamics, etc…

  

————————————————————

How to distinguish an SSV from the others

Note: SRVs only look like SSV5x

  

Their main traits are:

Round, circular build

Smooth material

Three claws

Spiked legs to immobilise targets

Large mouth that opens very wide

Shark like teeth that prevents the souls of the damned to escape

Long, flexible tongue that wraps the victim and takes their soul

Every SSV model

  

SSV1 was a lightweight yet somewhat reliable model that could do simple tasks like scan areas for soul energy. Their software was the easiest to edit, so all the other SSV-models that came after would share similar traits like determining whether or not the soul is an ally or a target, if it's strong enough to be a threat, etc.

  

After the colonisation of Gupar- the SSV1 model was repurposed to educate children and young teens on how magic worked and when interdimensional travel was invented, they were sent to other universes like Inero which soon caused ethical concerns within the Multiverse Trust Org with the council and politicians calling it too “Barbaric” to be used in colonisation and suggested they be revamped for self-defence and war.

  

This led to the creation of the SSV2 model which was an absolute mess- they couldn't properly absorb souls to heal themselves, only being able to attack things in small groups like gremlins to doggy pile, So instead, they were changed to be the SSV2 Mini, and the model took over, as a little extra storage unit for SSV1 models to contain souls. 

  

SSV3 or SSV1(PRO) was an absolute unit, it featured M-AI integration and could do the jobs of both SSV2 and SSV1 combined, but this made it pretty bulky, making it redundant in places where mobility and agility is required to get to targets. Thus, making it a useful tool for being attached to Mechs and used in Frontline warfare. It also featured a wide gaping jaw to suck in as much souls as possible. These were used to guard places and act as shields or tanks

  

SSV3-A was a varient of the SSV3, instead, being slimmer and inspired other war machines designers to go for aerodynamically flexible fighters. These didnt come with back up repairers or arms though, but did feature soul catalysts, a prototype catalyst type used to absorb different soul types and use it for energy

  

SSV4 Or SSVT(Titan) are bigger, badder, heavier. Acting as barricades to hallways or walls to cover other SSV models. It comes with 3 SSV2s for storage. This was a major success in many wars. It was also capable of turning into a ball to roll over tanks and other structures to pave the way. It’s body was similar to the shape of a round macaroon and its eyes were located on the head and torso, for 360 degree turning

  

SSV4.5(Carnage) were effective, using the SSV3-A slim build alongside the ability to use and manipulate artificial magic like nanotech, cyber implants, etc. It also could integrate some nanotechnology into arc materials and basically use the arc matter as if it were a mage- proving how easy it was for machines to manipulate traditions

  

SSV5 and its sibling models like X,S and U was the last of the SS series before the models started being revamped to SRV(soul rescue) models after the wars settled down.

  

The SSV5 Model was the ideal mobility SS, elegant, thin but sharp model allowed to to be deployed in facilities with SSV1s and were crafty, known to use their M-AI models to build soul storage units and were the most efficient of their Vs. The plating on the 5 and other 5 variants allowed it to repair itself via contact with certain materials. These bots were mass produced in the thousands before being shut down due to unknown reasons, however, it is speculated that a raid took place in the larger mass production factories.

  
  

SSV5 sibling models

  

SSV5x - A more robust and durable model, it was used to tank off the enemies frontlines whilst evading missiles and overall being a badass robot. Downside - Can’t contain too many souls otherwise it will self-destruct.

  

SSV5s - Mages worst enemies- these powerful bots were equipped with arc weapons, meaning it was very devastating in many environments where magic is scarce, as it could also leverage its souls arc type to produce the arc energy. Downside - Environment specific, meaning its arc weapons only work in their intended areas.

  

SSV5u - The ultimate SS model ever created- however, many rumours spread about how it was too “powerful” and a hacker known as [000] Script corrupted the model. This corruption lead its chassis to turn green and it lost control of itself. It was said to have “Taken the soul of a child” before being euthanized and discarded in a bin. There was only 1 SSV5U model ever created. Downside - No data.

After the wars

  
  

SRV models were used as peace machines who were placed in areas with chaos and destruction. These bots were known as the SSV terminators, as the companies who designed them had decided to make them useful for terminating SSV models with their SSV5 like abilities and reliability of healing using arc matter instead of direct souls

  

SSV models had been left to rot in facilities, with SSV3s being left as security and the others roamed around aimlessly in forests and urban areas. However, the recent uprising in riots from criminal gangs hijacking them had led to the release of hundreds- if not thousands of the monstrous SSV models.
