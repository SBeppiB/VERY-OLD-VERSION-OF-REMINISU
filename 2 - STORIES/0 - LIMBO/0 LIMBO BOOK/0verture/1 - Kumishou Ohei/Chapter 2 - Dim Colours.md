Tiny nanoscopic nanites crawled around his body, enveloping his inmate uniform and he felt as it reinforced itself, sorting out the layers and mechanisms. However, for whatever reason, it prevented him from moving.

Fido: “Just another damn malfunction, no wonder it felt weird” his mind spoke to him, finally finding a reason to brush off the awful feeling of something bad. 

He still felt unease as the systems inside the E.N.D activated one by one. Feeling the warmth of the E.N.D grow inside, he tried to move his arms around to let some of the heat off, but they felt too heavy, sluggish. 

Then, something struck, the E.N.D fulfilled its anagram of a name, and responded to his sudden movements. It heated up more, the feeling of tiny bugs creeping around him intensified. The nanobots were working. The technicians looked at him, with a look of disappointment. 

Some were smirking at the back, then Fido realised it was one of those tests. His life, gone too fast. A tingly chill slithered down his spine. He glanced at the technicians one more time, through their smug glass walls, watching him  every move was monitored for more than just data.

Fido’s pulse quickened as the suit pulsed with his panic, lights flickering like warning signs.. The voice of the technician crackled through the intercom.

Technician: “Initiating phase two. Prepare for engagement.”

Fido: “Engagement? The fuck do you mean engagement? Am I going to go to war???” his voice wavered but before he could protest, something shifted. 

The mech suit, which had been designed to protect, now felt like an instrument of torture. The first jolt was a sharp shock, and then another followed, sending a wave of adrenaline crashing through him. 

But the more he struggled, the more the suit seemed to tighten around him. He was bound by a force he couldn’t fight. His body, which had once been free, now felt like it was being crushed under the weight of this infernal machine. His vision blurred, and he could feel the temperature rising inside the suit.

A high-pitched sound rang in his ears, and then, without warning, sharp metal barbs shot out from the suit’s frame. They punctured his chest with brutal precision. It was piercing his body, like being stabbed by a thousand searing hot knives at once

Pain shot through him like lightning. But it wasn't just the spikes - something ELSE was wrong. His blood felt like it was boiling, skin crawling with invisible ants. Fido: "Wh-what's—" The nanobots were MULTIPLYING. Spreading through his veins.

Robert: "Immune response activated. Nanobot defensive protocols engaged. Commencing soul extraction while the body's distracted fighting itself."

Technician: "Sir, he's - he's literally being eaten alive from the inside—"

Robert: "That's the point. The pain keeps the soul anchored while we extract it. Beautiful, isn't it?"  blood spewed and sputtered like being shot out of a pressurised water jet. Fido gasped for breath, but his body wouldn’t obey. He was trapped, immobilized within the very machine he thought would save him, choking on his own blood.

As life drained out of him, Fido’s last thought dripped with bitter irony. 

Fido: "They were never testing the suit. They were testing me."

He felt bugs crawling in his lungs, suffocating him and Fido was slowly drowning. Not in water, but in shame. How could the great rebel of Pilton Dynamics fall to such a lowly bear trap? 

With every single drop of whatever strength he had left, he faced the tinted glass

Fido: “F- *cough* Fuck you” blood and black particles spewed out of his throat as his final words echoed throughout the chamber.
