Fido stumbled through the portal, stomach flipping like a dumpster fire on a rollercoaster. Colors bled into each other—greens that screamed, purples that reeked of rust, shadows that tickled his brain. Buddi’s voice tried to calm him, but it sounded like it came from underwater inside a blender. His visor tightened, loosened, tightened again,  mocking him. Each step felt like walking through syrup made of his own panic. The portal's energy readings spiked beyond anything Buddi had calculated. The nanobots in his suit screamed warnings as they detected impossible coordinates: 0, 0, 0, -0.

He wasn't traveling to another place. He was falling between realities, into the gaps that shouldn't exist.

Fido: “Buddi, where are we?”

Buddi: “Unknown. The dimensional readings are… impossible. We are located in a space between realities” The walls pulsed like lungs, exhaling dust and faint sparks.

Fido: “Buddi, scan for exit points.”
  
Buddi: “Scanning… no known exit."

 Fido: “Great. Trapped in cosmic soup. Cheers,”

Fido stumbled forward, but the floor melted under his boots like molasses. His arms flailed, hitting nothing, and for a second he was convinced the floor had eaten his leg. Nanobots spewed out of his hips, then glowed bright blue.

Buddi: “Sending nanobots to read stability and elevation”

 Buddi’s voice hummed in his ear, trying to calm him:

Buddi: “Stability readings… unreliable. Recommend cautious movement.”

Fido: “Yeah no shit?”

A faint crack appeared in the air again. Fido poked it with a trembling, charred pinkie. It hissed and sizzled like angry electricity.

Fido: “AGH—FUCK!”

Smoke curled around his hand. The smell was like burnt wires mixed with… old pizza. He yanked it back, muttering violent curses.

The darkness around him shifted, his footsteps echoed louder and abruptly went silent, his arms felt heavier than usual but his legs were spry, the lack of light led to Fido’s mind playing more tricks on him. It was almost sickening, the nausea and imaginary flashing lights whenever he closed his eyes ticked him off slowly. 

Buddi: “It appears you are currently experiencing the “Phosphene” effect. Adjusting visor…” The tight visor on Fido’s old face loosened a bit, not enough to see his nose but enough for comfort. And sometimes that was something rare that he could have once in a blue moon.  

Fido: “Buddi, you better not start calculating my death again.” 

Buddi: “Probability: 0.0003%.”

Fido: “0.0003%? That’s basically ‘you’re screwed.’ Thanks.”

The mirrors screamed into existence. Every reflection was light blue, and slightly faded, like ghosts. But all of them had the same thing- that same damn core in the middle of their chest. All of them looked just like him- same suit, same body type- minus a few girls. Some's faces were identical to his.  One Fido had jagged scars running down his arms like lightning strikes, eyes empty like forgotten files. Another was female, hair long and red, staring at him like she knew his deepest shame. A third had a mech devil tail attached to his coccyx, moving independently, twitching like a living nightmare. Fido blinked. They blinked back. One laughed—a high, sharp sound that shredded his nerves. “What the hell…?” he muttered, clutching his arm.

Fido looked around cautiously and only saw some blurred images of people and as he adjusted to the dim dark room, he could finally see the girl he saw earlier.  

Fido: "Who are you and where the hell am I?" His voice dropped low and confused

A voice boomed from above, seemingly close but somehow sounded  it were on the other side of the world

Voice:  "We have enough players now. Welcome To The LIMBO Void. There will be 8 players. Here, we will play 3 games. Each game will represent a crucial part of your lives, all with the same themes. Since your deaths were all similar- you all have been selected to come here to participate.” the voice echoed, without error. almost like it rehearsed this many times before. It was soft yet glassy, fragile yet hard. Almost female but almost not human.

Fido: “Eight of me? Cus I saw like other me’s…”

Voice: “These other “you’s” do not exist” 

Fido: “Bitch I know I’m not hallucinating”

The mirrors shattered. Light flooded the space, revealing a few other figures sprawled across the non-floor like discarded toys. 

Fido: “Wait what where’s-” he looked around, trying to find his doppelgangers

Fido remembered that he got killed, sudden shock waves go through his body as he remembers what happened. "*56927*", that's what his serial number was back then. 

Fido: “Buddi, tell me that gravity isn’t flipping again.”

Buddi: “It’s negotiating with itself. Unlikely to honor your request.”

Fido: “Fucking brilliant. I love being a science experiment.” his head thudded, and he almost fainted. The memories came back- but not even fully there. It’s like trying to imagine what another person went through but on substances

He punched at the wall, but then his fist slowly got dragged down, until he was fully on the floor, face practically smooshed against the wall. A blue lightning bolt zapped him before a gust of wind shot him up

Fido: "OH FUCK" he rolled violently in the air, heart racing and he tried twisting his body

A girl with white hair and red eyes sat up first, maybe fourteen, dressed in torn purple fabric that looked like it belonged in some fucked-up fairy tale. She stared at Fido with recognition that made his skin crawl.

Girl: "You're him. The one who broke everything."

Fido: "Lady, I don't even know where 'here' is."

Girl: “My name is Xia”

Fido: “You’re Chinese?”

Unnamed Voice: “That's racist!”

A man in military gear hauled himself upright next. The same voice who called Fido out on racism. Dark skin, eyepatch, more scars than flesh showing. His movements were too precise, too controlled—like someone who'd spent years having violence beaten into muscle memory. Fido turned around to look at Xia, only to realise she just vanished without a trace

Military Guy: "Name's Nexer. Former MSTF captain until I got my squad killed in a botched raid."  He turned to Fido, part of his face sliced but neatly healed by a scar. "And if you're all seeing what I'm seeing, we're seriously fucked”

Nexer studied Fido's suit with professional interest. 

Nexer: “That tech's military grade. Grappling hook on the left arm… 7 segment arm extenders… hell- even a knee guard! Where'd you get it?"

Fido:  "it was a gift from dear old dad.” 

Nexer: “Family business, huh?" Nexer's eyepatch caught the dim light. "mine disowned me after I got half my unit killed in a Guparian ambush. turns out following orders blindly makes you real popular with the brass, real unpopular with the families of dead soldiers" 

Xia: “You talk too much, soldier boy.”

Nexer: “And you disappear too much. We’re balanced.”

Xia wrapped her arms around herself.

Fido: “You’re just gonna take it like that? Classic disappearing ghost move.”

Xia: “I don’t disappear. I just don’t waste words on idiots.” Her dark crimson left eye glared at him through her fringe, her right violet eye covered.  

Fido: “…Point taken, lady ghost.”

Xia: “...”

The third figure—a young man, maybe early twenties, stood up. But he wasn't on the floor; but was on the ceiling. Clutching what looked like a broken tablet. His hands shook as he tried to power it on, panic written across his face. He looked up from his broken tablet. 

Tech guy: "At least you had orders. I was freelancing on reactor maintenance with my colleague Nick when the quantum core went critical." he looked nexer up and down "ugh, government assets. Really letting them use your body for their own conflicts?" 

Nexer inflated with a deep breath before cooling down

Fido: “You are…? And why the fuck are you sticking on the ceiling”

Tech Guy: "Alex. I was... I- Uhm…" His voice cracked. "The quantum readings here are impossible. We shouldn't exist in this space unless—"

Fido: “SHUT UP ALEX. NOBODY CARES. you're not even explaining anything USEFUL you're just saying "this is weird" which WE ALREADY KNOW”

Nexer: "...I think someone was stupid enough to build a portal without proper containment protocols," he finished, all eyes turning to Fido.

Fido: "Hey, my shit worked fine. This is something else."

Voice: "You will compete in three trials. Winners advance. The losers will be sent to hell, tortured for eternity."  she boomed again

Fido: “Hell?”

Four beds slid up through the floor like tombstones, each marked with a name: Fido, NEXER, XIA, ALEX, NICK

Alex: “Where's Nick…” he stared at his bed, and tried getting off the ceiling

Alex's tablet finally flickered to life, showing corrupted data streams.

Alex: "This is impossible. The dimensional mathematics doesn't work…" he shoves his hands in his scruffy hair

Nexer: "Kid, shut up with the science shit. We're trapped in some cosmic game show, and unless one of you knows how to fight our way out, we're gonna play by their rules." A long purple rod flew past them and phased through the wall. Nexer’s head turned to track it.

Fido looked at each of them—a traumatized girl with secrets, a dead soldier looking for redemption, and a panicked engineer trying to log his way out of hell.

Fido: "I know damn well I'm not dead. And if all of you are, then I don't know how I got here, but I sure as shit know I'm getting out." he tried to lean on the wall, only to be grabbed onto and strange yellow strings wrapped around him

His ears were shoved in too, and he could hear a conversation on the other side of the wall…  

Voice: “I- I had it with these people!”

Fido: “Who the fuck are you” his face was practically smooshed into the wall.
  
Voice: “HARK, WALL MAGE- FOR I AM THE MEDJAY OF THE DIAMOND DUNE REALM- AND I SHALL SMITE THY SOUL INTO OBLIVION- BEHOLD- MY TWO SWORDS, SPLAYER AND CHORBER.”

Fido: “Holy shit he named his swords” he giggled “I cant even see you paxxie, like- its just pitch black in here.”

Voice: “SILENCE! MY VOICE DEMANDS RESPECT- AND AS I SEE IT, THOU ART JEST UPON MY PRESENCE. FOR THAT- I WILL SEND THY SOUL TO LIMBO!”

Fido: “Sucks for you; I’m already in there.” He pulled his head out

Ming: The suit. It's that sideways eight ." he turns Emerald, quietly 

Emerald: "...you're certain?" she goes very still 

Ming: "Same design language as the weird block Same color scheme. He's connected to whatever sent us here."

Emerald: "Then we watch him. Closely."

The space around them defied physics in subtle, wrong ways. Shadows fell upward in some corners. Shadows crawled along walls that seemed to breathe. Beds floated three inches off the floor, trembling like they were alive. Fido reached out and touched one—his hand sank halfway through, chilling his veins. He jumped; gravity flipped for a second, sending his stomach into his throat.  

Fido: “What kind of cosmic acid trip is this?” he muttered, voice echoing in reverse.

Voice: "Rest. Tomorrow, the first trial begins.”

Fido; “Okay- too much happening at once.” Fido dragged himself toward the floating bed, muscles screaming like broken engines.

Nexer: “Careful where you step. Floors here don’t obey rules.” he says, as his boots slowly sank into the floor

Fido: “Funny, neither do mirrors. One of them just winked at me.”

Nexer: “You’re hallucinating.” he side-eyes him

Fido: “Yeah? My hallucinations are scarier than your eyepatch.”

Xia watched silently, lips pressed tight. Nexer leaned against a wall, eyepatch catching the dim light. Alex muttered numbers to himself like chanting. Fido lay down, visor fogged, thoughts spinning in a carnival of panic, sarcasm, and plotting. 
 
Xia: “Are we supposed to trust each other?”

Fido: “Trust? In a place where reality shits on physics? Nope.” he ragdolls on his bed

Nexer: “Agreed. Survival first. Ego later.”

Alex: “And data logs…”

Fido: “…fuck your logs.” he layed back on his bed, dry blood cracked under his head

Fido:” Tomorrow… I’m not losing. Not to some fuckass cosmic kids game.” he whispered to himself like a mantra
