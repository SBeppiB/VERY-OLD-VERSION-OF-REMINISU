A massive court case against Pilton Dynamics is underway - Valorin Karzad (Dr. Chen's wife) vs Rothenszein (corpo lawyer). Witnesses include Marie Scari (who reveals she tipped off Rena), Sarah Paulauskas (SSV-5U creator), and Lorenzo Hendrick (security chief). Mid-trial, portal anomaly detected at Facility 774. Cut to Kerra - elite anomaly hunter - who gets deployed with KAU-18 squad to investigate. SSV-5U (Nepherite) shows up and slaughters half her team (18 down to 7). Squad forced to jump through unstable portal to escape. Someone tries to be a hero, stays behind to change coordinates but gets clipped - portal sends Kerra's squad to Limbo by accident instead of intended destination.

Valorin is a widow with 34 years of rage, going against Rothenszein who's a cold corpo bastard twisting every fact. Marie is guilt-ridden and confesses to helping Rena.
