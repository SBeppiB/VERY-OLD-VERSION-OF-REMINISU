**OUTLINE**

They all settle into the abandoned warehouse after reuniting. Fido finds ominous writing on the walls and shrugs it off, whilst Karth recognizes that Fido might have seen his own ghost too cus of the finger claw mark matching Fido's. Everyone decides to pool up their technology. Zibib finds some tech that resembles Fido's and asks how they work, Fido lightly explains and lends some nanobots to Zibib (the exp-3 one). Zibib integrates it into her skin and gets amazed. Emerald and Zibib form some kinda rivalry cus both are survivalists with different approaches. Xia just watches them destroy everything like its entertainment. Eventually they all become hungry and realise that food isn't something they can easily find. They spend months talking about themselves and fortifying the place.

Emerald and Zibib start questioning each other's survival methods, Xia just watches them as obviously pointed out earlier. Fido mocks Ming for having magic and showing that tech is really much better which pisses Ming off more.
