This chapter’s essentially what happened to facility 774 after Fido’s death and is the only chapter where Fido doesn’t star as the main character, instead its being his girlfriend- Rena Scari. This delves deeply into more of the world design and what happens around the local star systems.

Rena scari as the main character has a lot of connections to people outside pilton dynamics, except Marie, her cousin, who works as a scientist. She also was doing a little assassination mission before Marie called her. Her associates, the mages in Orinia, act tired of her but do need her money to keep buying more weapons for Rena.
