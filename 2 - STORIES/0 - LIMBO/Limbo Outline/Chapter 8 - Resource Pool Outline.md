

OUTLINE

After waking up the next morning following months of fortifying, the group realizes they need actual resources to survive long-term. Fido officially tries to help everyone by replicating his nanobots for the whole group after Zibib's success with the exp-3. They all pool up their remaining technology to see what they're working with. Ming continues being hostile towards Fido. The group plans their first major scavenging mission to find supplies and potentially other survivors.

Fido and Karth get along really well because they're pretty much just suffering together with everyone's mess. Ming still hates Fido. Emerald and Zibib's rivalry simmers but they cooperate for survival. Xia observes everything quietly.
