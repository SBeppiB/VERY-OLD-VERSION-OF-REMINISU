OUTLINE

Fido and Karth go on a scout mission to find the others and gather resources so they all survive and don't fuckin die. They scavenge abandoned convenience stores and find old snacks from the ancient days. In the convenience store, Karth tries a bite of old food, only to spit it out straight after. They continue searching and find Nexer and Alex in a subway stairwell, cornered by lab coat creatures on both sides. Alex hacks into a port in the lights and blinds everyone, helping the gang escape. They all dash back to another abandoned building next to a car garage to catch their breath before heading to the warehouse.

CHARACTER DYNAMICS 

Fido and Karth get along really well, suffering together during the mission. Alex and Fido sorta get along well when they meet up. Alex does annoy Karth a bit with his rambling. Nexer just tries to keep Alex focused and help with the escape. Alex starts rambling about how he can't fight and that his only way of offending is by hacking on stuff, Nexer slaps his head and tells him to focus.
