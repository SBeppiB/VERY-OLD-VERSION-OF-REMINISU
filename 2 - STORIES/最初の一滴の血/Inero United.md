                   ¤《Story Below》¤

  

In the Nimbus Metro City, a hazy mist looms over the skyscrapers as the sounds of oncoming traffic fills the air and the bustling and hustling of crowds getting their necessities from market places.

  

On the outskirts of the inner city, there lies an old bridge with a young boy sitting on a bench casually watching the dark red sunset. A faint sound of little mages and kids playing with magic echoes through the forests

  

He checks the time from his phone. “20:43”. He sighed and as he gets up to go home, he bumps into a girl. 

  

The boy: “OH I'm so sorry!”

  

The girl: “Ah it's alright.” She looks at his unusual clothing. “White lab coat outside here?” She thought to herself whilst noticing a nametag on his coat reading “Tosho’.

  

 Her eyes slightly widened after looking at him and seeing red stains on the back of his arms. 

  

The girl walks up behind him and taps on his shoulder. 

  

The girl: “Hey, you okay? You got some red stains on your coat ”

  

Tosho: “Hm? Oh this? This was used for uhm… something I forgot.”

  

The girl: “Are you sure you're not one of those Ethrax crackheads?” She teased at his fatigued posture.

  

Tosho: “Nah, I'm just a bit tired. I had a long day of work today”

  

The girl: “Right, you really don't look so good- are you sure you're okay?”

  

Tosho looked at the girl for a few seconds and had sudden pain in his back then collapsed onto the floor

  

Tosho: “Well I don't think I'm okay” he says whilst grasping his right shoulder tightly. He tries to stand up but stumbled on the girl 

  

The girl: “Oh, careful!” She helps him up by the shoulders. 

  

Tosho: “My head hurts”. He faints on the hard metal floor and the girl seemingly manages to carry him to her apartment. 

  

She aids him in knowing what Pilton Dynamics could've done if they find out he's recovering in a hospital not too far away. She searches up “Tosho” and the results show that he was in fact a nano technological theorist ID #43.

  

A few hours passed and the girl came to the conclusion that Tosho was abused by Pilton Dynamics. Looking at her monitor and back at the boy laying on her couch, she couldn't fathom how an innocent boy like him would be tortured, and soon enough, he woke up feeling groggy

  

Tosho: “Ugh, how long was I out for?”

  

The girl: “Few hours, hey, I got a question for you "Tosho" if that's your real name”

  

Tosho: “Yeah sure, by the way, I didn't catch your name?”

  

The girl: “...names Fia” she looked away for a second, before glaring right back

  

Fia: “Anways,why were you a documented “Willing Labour Volunteer” in Pilton Dynamics? It sounds like you were a slave or something”

  

Tosho hesitated for a moment, looking outside and tries to catch his breath

  

Tosho: “ I don't remember much about it, I do remember however, that they told me I was daydreaming on the job and let me out to get a break. They did look a bit off though”

  

Fia: “And you didn't give them an extra thought? Not even a little “Damn, were they giving me anesthetics whilst I was daydreaming, thus my weird memory gaps” or something”

  

Tosho glared at her and started contemplating what happened

  

Tosho: “Well, whatever they did couldnt have been THAT bad, maybe you're overthinking it? I mean- I don't really feel pain”

  

Fia: “That literally contradicts what happened a few hours ago. Remember when you literally fainted on me?”

  

Tosho: “It didnt mean anything I swea-”

  

Fia: “I barely know you why would I think of it like that” she squinted

  

Tosho: “Well- uh, I, uhm-”

  

Fia: “Hey I was just pullin’ your leg!” she giggles.

  

Fia: “Anyways, I think you’re well enough to go back home, lemme grab my keys and you can be on your way.”

  

Tosho: “Oh, right, okay. Home…” he stared into a plant pot in a trance like state

  

The plant was all he could focus on, its strange pattern on its leaves were confusing yet calming at the same time. “Am I high?” Tosho pondered to himself whilst trying to unlock his gaze upon this plant
