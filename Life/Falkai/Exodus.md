The Falkai Exodus slip into prisons at midnight, freeing political prisoners, arming them with stolen blades, and disappearing before sunrise. The chaos starves regimes of order. If prisoners riot and die — good. If they survive and sow terror — better.

They spread false prophecies about tyrants choking on their own sins. Once belief takes root, Exodus makes it real, matching the rumors down to every dying breath.

They train Wraith Speakers — spies who mimic the voices of dead heroes to incite mobs. The ghosts whisper vengeance, and the crowds obey, tearing walls down for nothing but rage.

Exodus crafts arc resonance bombs — not loud, but sinister. One pulse wipes clean the memories in town squares, leaving regimes grasping for control as the people forget their lies.

The elders of Exodus accept revenge contracts from the poorest and the broken — no gold, just a blood payment. A single drop spilled in oath can buy a king’s death, if Exodus wills it.
