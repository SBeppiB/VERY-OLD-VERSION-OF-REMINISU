  
Requiem


The Falkai Requiem was all about assassinating those in higher power with blood bites and other hidden blade variants, usually to take more control over smaller factions and cults. These ones used assassin raids or brute force to get what they wanted. they're left handed

  

At first, they stalk and gain information, then slowly build up tension as their members corroded the governments from the inside, only for them to break it down

  

Requiem does this to usually get valuable items or Hexmals, typically to empower themselves and the other faction of the shrouds

  

Falkai Requiem expands their territory and reaches by staging ritual murders inside sacred temples, corrupted churches and royal halls, leaving hex-scripted curses which melt the heirs sanity and force entire bloodlines to rot from the inside.They secretly kidnap illegitimate children of nobles and raise them as hidden moles within cities and courts. If a mole fails their mission, they’re slaughtered and fed to the blade. If they succeed, they slit new throats in turn.

They keep forbidden libraries full of spell-tech blueprints, with the dead bodies of archivists embalmed as living batteries to power Bloodbite upgrades. Requiem runs fake cults to bait desperate rebels — once a mob gathers and trusts them, Requiem burns them alive and pins it on the old regime to spread fear like wildfire.

Every lunar eclipse, Requiem’s leaders duel each other near to death. The winner drinks the loser’s blood to bind their memories and skill into their veins, passing down pain like a sacred heirloom.
