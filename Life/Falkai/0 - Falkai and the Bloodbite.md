General Cult initiation


All new Shroud initiates must undergo these rituals to prove and mark their spot in the Falkai shrouds

This is the General cult shit

Before entering the shroud, they must do the first step- Shroud Rites.

They must sew their own hood and cloak from white cloths, then bleed onto it from a cut across their face, specifically below the cheekbone. Dye lasts forever. If the cuts are messy? The redder the stain, the more devotion, the more respect. This will always remind them of Iron and what they devoted their lives to, and they cannot wash it off.

After that, they will commit to a whisper vigil, where they meditate overnight in a chamber lined with magical residue of the souls of the damned. They whisper the shroud’s creed, and how much they will give up for the Falkai. These echos will “carry” them to the Shroud’s goddess. The next sunrise, if they’re silent, they will go to Exodus. If they are fueled by some “unknown anger”, they will go to Requiem

On that same day, at exactly noon, they will mix wax with their blood to make candles. Initiates must swear their loyalty by the candle’s flame. If they break the oath, the candle will haunt you. No idea how, it just will.

At night, they must painstakingly craft their own mask out of rare materials and make sure its able to fit on their face properly. If not? Redo everything. If it fits? They’re part of the shroud

The masks represent the new faces they will wear, not frowning, not scared, always smiling, always watching.
