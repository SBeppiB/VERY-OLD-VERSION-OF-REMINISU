Humanoids (species that look or resemble a human shape)

  

Humans - Basic people with weak souls. Very strong willed and curious beings. Basically zero 
They have very strong bodies (compared to some other species)


Mages - Emotional jesters with strong souls. Uses magic in day to day life
Weaker bodies, can live longer

  

Magicians - Wannabe Mages. Kinda emo at times of darkness
Stronger than mages, weaker than humans. Their souls aren't as strong as mages, but its equipped for hex casting
  

Guparians - Imagine a Parasite and mage/Magician. Very hostile

  

Aezorian - Advanced species, rival of guparians, strongest souls. Allied with humans. Has the weakest bodies, requires a shell to even touch physical objects

Ethernix - giants with a tendancy to consume sunlight
Shrinks when starved
Colour changes
Red star - Red body
Orange star - Orange body
Yellow star - Yellow body
Purple star - Purple body
Blue star - Blue body
White star - White body

  

Eketoplasian - A type of magician, however, more ghostly, beings in the realm of life and death

  

Phaezernix - Meta-dashers, Bots, Cyborgs who all work under a bounty hunting contract in V-space

  

Aetherians - 0% energy wasted Solar powered Hybrids between Ethernix and Aezorians
