

In Tales From The Cosmos, there lies a cold field. A war, hate without motives. Conflicts are at an all time high as people fight for power—to find purpose in life, to fill the void in their soul. Will they find the meaning of life? Or was it all for nothing?

In the years 3421-3561, war consumed the omniverse. The Boomin Burst had already unified the cosmic mesh, connecting the afterlife and condensing reality into a volatile new structure. But the megaverses—massive territorial zones within this unified space—remained distinct enough to breed conflict.

Azuria, a self-proclaimed megaverse on the outer rim, waged war against Yezuriat, the megaverse housing the densest concentration of Prime AU variants. With the burst having eliminated countless non-prime timelines and merged alternate versions into singular prime existences, overpopulation became catastrophic. Too many prime souls, too little space, too much power concentrated in to few hands.

The surviving Prime AU inhabitants—the "true" versions of beings who'd absorbed or outlasted their alternate selves—formed factions. They clashed over territory, resources, and the fundamental question of purpose in a post-burst reality where even death had been restructured.

Then in 3452, Protocol FR-32 was activated.

The Omni-book's ancient failsafe—designed to trigger when reality became "too merged"—began violently re-slicing timelines. But this time, something went wrong. Instead of separating realities, it caused a cascading collapse. Stray timeline fragments that had barely survived the original burst now imploded entirely, their matter and souls compressing into the Prime AU spaces.

Eco watched this chaos and decided to intervene—not to help, but to simplify. "Life has enough space to move around," it declared, and with a single reality edit, forced every remaining AU pocket to collapse inward. All variants, all branches, all timeline remnants—compressed into Origin, now renamed Singularity for the unified point it had become.

  

Because of this, non Prime characters were quickly eradicated, as they were inferior to their prime versions, and AU Prime characters were forced into Limbo, where all they would do is fight to be the “True” AU Prime versions of themselves, only to sacrifice for the Announcer to feast on them. Eventually, the ones who were redeemed “failures” were set free, only to die eventually due to factions overpowering other beings. 

Solo adventurers like Fido Pilton and Xia survived this whole disaster, due to their abilities to resurrect after death, and sheer skill. The afterlife was destroyed, so souls who were meant to go somewhere other than Limbo were forced into a place of “constant and eternal suffering”. Due to this, SBeppi and The balance leave the lemniscate to try and recover them, as they are important to keep the omniverse alive, as well as to make them colonise the 4th and 5th dimension for some reasons…???

Anyways, with the big 6 leaving the picture, chaos ensued within the lemniscate. Without their creators, all hope was lost. The remaining Prime AU’s formed together to raise hell upon the outer rim of Origin, which changed its name to Singularity. Soon, over many years, their technology was too advanced. The strongest craved for more, and so, they used the SoulSnatcher Variants to eradicate almost all mages, life, arcanium, etc. They effectively stole magic from its source, mining every chunk of dark matter, as well as making new magics. 

  

The Mages and Magicians however, didn't like this- so they made their own versions of the SSV models. “Sanguine Paladins” they called it, a suit of armour that would be able to tank the non mages attacks, and heal efficiently off of arc matter. Alongside this, the most powerful hexmal users were using the SP armours to wipe out many planets. 74 galaxies collapsed later, blood fueled, soul fueled, arc fueled, hell- even dark magic fueled machines and armour were littered across space.

Eventually, false idols quickly grew like wildfire; manipulating what is real and what is fake. People yearned for more though, and broke through the outer rim, exposed to True Infinity. They developed new technology to venture into the True Infinity and colonised it, and its almost infinite amount of AU's and Timelines.
