Biology

Z-blood interacts with arcanium matter since it's like an aura that allows the user to absorb and harness the energy as previously mentioned. Souls can handle many magic types but often takes a toll on the body as they become more fragile.

  

Humans have H-Blood (Human blood) which isn't really useful for spell casting
