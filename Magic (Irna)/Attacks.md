Mages and Magicians both use casting circles which show the symbol of the magic they're using, this is because using magic straight from the vessel can use a lot of their mana, as there’s no protection from the dense arc matter. Magicians can also choose between Spell Cards, hex weaving, arc glyphs, etc.

  

Magicians and mages other main difference is the fact that Magicians only cast curses and ailments whilst mages can only conjure their surroundings based on their magic type. Speaking of, Elemental magic users or mages can only use their magic if there's any substance around them that they can use like an earth mage lifting a boulder with their magic.

  

All magic users cannot harm themselves with their own attacks/magic and are also less affected by their elements like a fire mage walking on fire. Same applies to for example, lightning mage striking another lightning mage and the victim taking less damage, it however, can be affected by how old and technique the casting is used. Magic extends one's life at the cost of them being more fragile and physically weak, but they can substitute magic prosthetics for that like an ice arm or rock leg with their respective Magics.

  

Magics have branches like Earth having Sand magic, for some reason and learning how to master a branch is like learning a piano, without eyes. It's pretty hard if you don't have assistance from a person of that branch. Learning another magic type is harder-er (is that a word?) and usually comes from Awakenings in temples. Some other methods of gaining a new magic type is to expose yourself in that magic dense environment for long amounts of time like a year long.
