Arcanium/Arc matter is the fundamental resource used to allow mages/magicians to use....magic. It is an invisible form of fluid matter. Pure arcanium "infects" the object its attached to and allows mages to control it using hooks from their Z-blood/bio aura. 

Humans cannot sense it with your natural five senses, but mages and people with a bio aura can sense it via their "third eye", which lets them see it with their normal two eyes if they put a bit of focus into it.


It's nearly impossible to make arcanium, since its atomic properties are too precise to be replicated in labs. However, arcanium is a material that's nearly-infinite in the centre of the Origin universe, with jets and streams of nonillions of tonnes being ejected every second. An exception to this is the Arcanium Hexmal, which lets the user to create pure arcanium out of nowhere.

Arcanium Energy can be described as waves with frequencies. Each frequency is assigned to different elements and on the waves, there are little physical “hooks” that can be affected by the mage's own soul as mentioned before

  

Casting can be used in two ways for Elemental mages. 

  

1. Pure - Directly from the soul. Mages will absorb some of their own elements into their souls and expel it out of their hands or arms or whatever body part they want it to be casted out of. This gives it time to be radioactive with the energy and giving it more projectile power

  
  

2. Raw - Picked up without proper time to harness it. Mages will run their hands/body part whatever they use to cast through the matter they can use and cast directly from the source(s). Since they didn't properly harness, they can't shoot it fast, meaning they have to rely on their own hands to pick it up and throw it.
