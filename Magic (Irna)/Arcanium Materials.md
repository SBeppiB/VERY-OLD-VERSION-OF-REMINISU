Magic Gems and Crystals

  

Magic is often imbued into gems, crystals and sometimes certain metals and takes the elemental power of nearby magic types, like a diamond being surrounded by water magic makes a water diamond

  

Mages use these special materials to harness their magics through weapons and items, like a fire mage using a fire imbued dagger to deal fire damage more effectively, opposed to just scalding a dagger with magic.
